## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Don't rush** - Speak clearly and at moderate pace
❌ **Don't apologize** - If something looks different, keep going
❌ **Don't show errors** - If something fails, stop and restart
❌ **Don't use real patient data** - HIPAA violation!
❌ **Don't mention bugs or "coming soon"** features
❌ **Don't ramble** - Stick to the script
❌ **Don't have notifications pop up** - Turn off ALL notifications
