# RealDiag Demo Video Script
## Professional Clinical Decision Support Demo (5-7 minutes)

---

## 🎬 Production Notes

**Target Audience:** Healthcare professionals, hospital administrators, potential investors
**Video Length:** 5-7 minutes
**Tone:** Professional, clinical, evidence-based
**Format:** Screen recording with voiceover
**Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p)
**Frame Rate:** 30fps

### Recording Tools:
- **Screen Recorder:** OBS Studio, Camtasia, or ScreenFlow
- **Audio:** Clear USB microphone or headset
- **Editing:** DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, or Final Cut Pro

---

## 📋 Pre-Recording Checklist

- [ ] Clear browser cache and restart browser
- [ ] Open https://realdiag.netlify.app in Chrome/Edge
- [ ] Close all unnecessary browser tabs
- [ ] Disable browser notifications
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 100%
- [ ] Hide bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B)
- [ ] Prepare sample patient scenarios
- [ ] Test microphone audio levels
- [ ] Close Slack, email, and other distracting apps
- [ ] Use "Do Not Disturb" mode on computer

---

## 🎯 Video Structure

### Act 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:45)
### Act 2: Core Features (0:45 - 3:00)
### Act 3: Advanced Features (3:00 - 5:30)
### Act 4: Integration & Conclusion (5:30 - 7:00)

---

## 📝 Full Script with Timing

---

### **ACT 1: INTRODUCTION** (0:00 - 0:45)

#### VISUAL: Landing Page
**[Screen shows RealDiag homepage with logo prominently displayed]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Welcome to RealDiag - an AI-powered, real-time diagnostic assistant designed for healthcare professionals. RealDiag combines evidence-based clinical decision trees with intelligent symptom analysis to support accurate, efficient diagnosis at the point of care."

**[Pause 2 seconds to show the landing page]**

> "With over 400 diagnoses across 24 medical specialties, RealDiag provides comprehensive clinical decision support backed by guidelines from ACC, AHA, ADA, IDSA, KDIGO, and other leading medical organizations."

**[Mouse hovers over navigation to show available sections]**

---

### **ACT 2: CORE FEATURES** (0:45 - 3:00)

---

#### SCENE 1: Symptom-Based Search (0:45 - 2:00)

**[Click on "Symptom Search" from navigation]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Let's start with our symptom-based diagnostic search. This is where most clinicians begin their diagnostic journey."

**[Screen shows empty symptom search page]**

> "I'll demonstrate with a common emergency department presentation: a patient with chest pain."

**[Type "chest pain" in the search box and press Enter]**

**ACTION:**
1. Type: "chest pain"
2. Press Enter
3. **PAUSE** - Let autocomplete appear
4. Add symptom: "shortness of breath"
5. Add symptom: "diaphoresis"
6. Add symptom: "nausea"

**VOICEOVER (while adding symptoms):**
> "The interface provides intelligent autocomplete, helping clinicians quickly enter symptoms using standardized medical terminology. Let me add a few more presenting symptoms: shortness of breath, diaphoresis, and nausea."

**[Click "Search" button]**
**[Wait for results to load - 2-3 seconds]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "RealDiag instantly searches across all diagnostic rules and returns ranked differential diagnoses based on symptom matching."

---

#### SCENE 2: Results & Scoring (2:00 - 2:45)

**[Results appear - scroll slowly through top 3-4 results]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Each diagnosis is scored on a 0-10 scale, combining multiple factors: symptom match, sensitivity, specificity, and prevalence. The top result here is Acute Coronary Syndrome with a match score of 9.2 out of 10."

**[Click to expand the first result card - ACS or STEMI]**

**ACTION:**
1. Click the first diagnosis card to expand it
2. **PAUSE** for 2 seconds to show the expanded content
3. Slowly scroll through the sections

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Each diagnosis card provides comprehensive clinical information. We see the ICD-10 code, LOINC codes for relevant lab tests, and most importantly - clinical pearls for bedside decision making."

**[Point to or highlight the Clinical Pearls section]**

> "Clinical pearls offer practical insights like 'Troponin may not elevate until 3-6 hours after symptom onset' - critical knowledge that can prevent missed diagnoses."

**[Scroll to Management section]**

> "The management section provides evidence-based treatment protocols, including antiplatelet therapy, anticoagulation, and the need for cardiology consultation."

**[Scroll to Recommended Tests section]**

> "And here are the recommended diagnostic tests, including ECG, cardiac biomarkers, and imaging studies - all linked to LOINC codes for easy ordering."

---

#### SCENE 3: Differential Diagnosis Comparison (2:45 - 3:00)

**[Scroll back up to see multiple results]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Notice how other life-threatening conditions are also ranked highly: Pulmonary Embolism, Aortic Dissection, Pneumothorax - ensuring critical diagnoses aren't missed."

**[Hover over 2-3 different diagnosis cards to show variety]**

---

### **ACT 3: ADVANCED FEATURES** (3:00 - 5:30)

---

#### SCENE 4: Red Flag Alerts (3:00 - 3:30)

**[Scroll to a diagnosis with red flag alerts - should be visible on ACS card]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "RealDiag includes advanced clinical decision support features. For time-sensitive conditions, you'll see red flag alerts with pulsing borders."

**[Point to the red flag alert section]**

**ACTION:**
1. Expand the Red Flag Alerts section if collapsed
2. **PAUSE** for 3 seconds
3. Slowly read through the critical actions

**VOICEOVER:**
> "These alerts highlight critical actions that must be taken immediately, time windows for intervention, and mortality risk data. For acute coronary syndrome, we see the 'door-to-balloon' time of 90 minutes and the dramatic increase in mortality with treatment delays."

---

#### SCENE 5: Clinical Decision Calculators (3:30 - 4:15)

**[Scroll up to find the Clinical Decision Calculators section]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "RealDiag integrates validated clinical decision calculators directly into the diagnostic workflow."

**[Click to expand the calculators section]**

**ACTION:**
1. Expand "Clinical Decision Calculators" section
2. Scroll through the available calculators
3. Click on "HEART Score" or "TIMI Score"

**VOICEOVER:**
> "For our chest pain patient, relevant calculators include the HEART score for cardiac risk stratification, TIMI score for acute coronary syndrome, and Wells criteria for pulmonary embolism."

**[If time allows, show calculator interface]**

> "These calculators help quantify risk and guide disposition decisions - admission versus discharge, observation versus intervention."

---

#### SCENE 6: Drug Interactions & Cost-Effectiveness (4:15 - 5:00)

**[Scroll to find Drug Interactions or Cost-Effectiveness section]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "The system also checks for potential drug interactions when multiple medications are recommended in the treatment plan."

**[Show drug interaction warnings if available]**

**ACTION:**
1. Expand Drug Interactions section
2. Show severity ratings and alternatives
3. Briefly show Cost-Effectiveness section if present

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Each interaction is rated by severity with clear alternatives and monitoring recommendations. Additionally, cost-effectiveness analysis compares diagnostic pathways, helping providers choose the most efficient workup strategy while maintaining diagnostic accuracy."

---

#### SCENE 7: Mobile & Offline Features (5:00 - 5:30)

**[Click "Enable Mobile Features" button if visible, or mention them]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "For clinicians on the go, RealDiag includes mobile-optimized features including voice input for hands-free symptom entry, barcode scanning for rapid patient identification, and full offline functionality."

**[If showing mobile view, briefly demo voice or scanner]**

**ACTION:**
1. Click the mobile features toggle
2. Show voice input button
3. Show offline status indicator

**VOICEOVER:**
> "The entire diagnostic database can be downloaded for offline use - critical for rural settings or areas with unreliable internet connectivity."

---

### **ACT 4: EPIC INTEGRATION & CONCLUSION** (5:30 - 7:00)

---

#### SCENE 8: EHR Integration (5:30 - 6:00)

**[Navigate to Integration page or show integration diagram]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "RealDiag integrates seamlessly with Epic and other EHR systems using SMART on FHIR standards."

**[Show the integration page at /integration]**

**ACTION:**
1. Click "API" or "Integration" from navigation
2. Show FHIR integration diagram
3. Highlight automated data pull features

**VOICEOVER:**
> "When launched from within Epic, RealDiag automatically pulls patient demographics, active conditions, current medications, allergies, and recent lab results. This eliminates manual data entry and reduces the risk of errors."

**[Show or mention automated criteria matching]**

> "The system automatically evaluates lab values against diagnostic criteria - checking if troponin is elevated, if white blood cell count suggests infection, or if kidney function affects medication dosing."

---

#### SCENE 9: Clinical Workflow (6:00 - 6:30)

**[Return to symptom search or show workflow diagram]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "RealDiag fits naturally into clinical workflow. From the patient chart, clinicians can launch RealDiag with a single click. Enter symptoms, review differential diagnoses, check clinical pearls, and return to the EHR to place orders."

**[Optional: Show quick workflow demo]**

**ACTION:**
1. Show quick symptom entry
2. Show results
3. Mention returning to EHR

**VOICEOVER:**
> "The entire process takes 60-90 seconds, providing immediate clinical decision support without disrupting patient care."

---

#### SCENE 10: Account & Pricing (6:30 - 6:45)

**[Click on Account page or show pricing]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "RealDiag offers flexible subscription options for individual clinicians, medical groups, and healthcare organizations."

**[Briefly show the pricing page]**

**ACTION:**
1. Navigate to /pricing
2. Briefly show the three tiers
3. Don't linger - move quickly

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Plans start at 29 dollars per month for individual clinicians, with volume discounts available for organizations. RealDiag employees with @realdiag.org email addresses receive complimentary full access."

---

### **CLOSING** (6:45 - 7:00)

**[Return to home page showing logo]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "RealDiag - AI-Powered Real-Time Diagnostic Assistant. Evidence-based clinical decision support at the point of care."

**[Show contact information or call to action]**

> "Visit realdiag.netlify.app to start your free trial, or contact us at support@realdiag.org for enterprise inquiries. Thank you for watching."

**[Fade to black with RealDiag logo and website URL]**

## 🎵 Audio Considerations

### Background Music:
- Professional, ambient music (low volume)
- Medical/corporate style (Epidemic Sound, AudioJungle)
- Should NOT overpower voiceover
- Suggested tracks: "Medical Documentary Theme" or "Healthcare Innovation"

### Voiceover:
- Clear, professional tone
- Moderate pace (not too fast)
- Emphasize key features
- Practice script multiple times before recording

### Sound Effects (optional, minimal):
- Subtle "whoosh" for transitions
- Soft click sounds for button presses (if tasteful)
- **Avoid:** Excessive sound effects that distract
